    How is this device used?
    ReaSet picks this for you when you open it — there is a Director already, or this device becomes one. Change it here only if you want a different device to own the setlist tonight.
    Director
    Owns the show: edits the setlist, its order, chains, loops and skips, and publishes them to every other device. One per session.
    Controller
    Drives the show: play, pause, previous, next, cue and queue. Follows the Director's setlist and cannot edit it. Any number of devices.
